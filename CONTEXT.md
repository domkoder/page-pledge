# PagePledge — Domain Glossary

- **Cohort** — A group of participants committing to the same learning goal under fixed rules, created via a **Natural-Language Prompt -> strict Zod Intent (`/api/intent`) -> Human-Readable Terms Card -> Confirm** flow (ADR 0012). Rules are immutable once created.
- **Milestone** — A dated learning checkpoint within a cohort (`2..10` milestones). Has a deadline, a cohort grace period, and is bound to the cohort's on-chain **`contentHash`** (`score >= 75` required to pass, max 3 on-chain commit attempts; ADR 0011, ADR 0012).
- **Material** — The reading passages for a cohort's milestones: defaults to 5 built-in original Rust Ownership & Borrowing passages with an optional creator-pasted custom study text toggle (ADR 0003, ADR 0012). Its SHA-256 `contentHash` is stored on-chain, and the AI Oracle grades comprehension strictly against this material (with prompt-injection protection, deterministic offline rubric fallback, and an off-chain **Adaptive Practice Card** targeting weak topics).
- **Pledge** — A participant's native SOL deposit into the cohort vault, tracked and calculated strictly in 9-decimal integer **lamports** (`1 SOL = 1_000_000_000n` lamports) across on-chain accounts, `ChainClient`, and `lib/economics.ts`, and formatted as SOL only at the UI boundary (ADR 0010).
- **Commit** — A participant's on-chain `commit_submission(milestone_idx, answer_hash)` transaction recorded before `deadline[i]` (max 3 attempts per milestone). Proves on-time submission and binds the subsequent oracle attestation to that exact `answer_hash` (ADR 0011).
- **Forfeiture** — The portion of a pledge lost when a participant has `missed_i` missed milestones at cohort settlement: `F_i = floor(stake * missed_i / M)` (`1/M` of stake per missed milestone; ADR 0013). Deterministic; never decided by the oracle.
- **Neutral outcome (`NEUTRAL`)** — Deterministic liveness fallback when a participant committed before `deadline[i]` but the oracle had zero activity through `deadline[i] + grace`: the participant suffers no forfeiture for that milestone, but is ineligible for the reward pool. Supersedes manual creator overrides with zero trust assumptions (ADR 0011).
- **Keeper bounty** — Permissionless incentive paid to whoever calls `settle_user` for a participant: `bounty_i = min(floor(F_i * 100 / 10_000), 10_000_000n)` lamports (`1%` of forfeit, capped at `0.01 SOL`), deducted from `F_i` to leave net forfeit `N_i = F_i - bounty_i` (ADR 0013).
- **Protocol fee & Treasury** — Fixed `200 bps` (`2.00%`) of total net forfeits (`sum(N_i)`), plus integer rounding dust (and any simulated yield when nobody is eligible), routed to the protocol Treasury so the solvency invariant `sum(payouts) + fee + dust == A` holds to the exact lamport (ADR 0013).
- **Reward pool** — Net forfeits after the 200 bps protocol fee (plus any yield when `h == 1`), distributed pro-rata by stake to eligible finishers who `PASSED` all `M` milestones (or refunded `floor(h * (1 - phi) * N_i)` per user if nobody finished all `M` milestones; ADR 0013).
- **Oracle** — The single off-chain key that signs **completion attestations** after the AI grades a quiz. It can approve; it can never slash (ADR 0001).
- **Completion attestation** — An oracle-signed Ed25519 statement over `"PAGEPLEDGE_ATT_V1" || programId || clusterId || cohort || cohortEpoch || user || milestoneIdx || score || commitHash || expiresAt` verifying that participant `user` passed `milestoneIdx` for their latest `commitHash`, verified on-chain via the Ed25519 precompile and updating `cohort.last_oracle_ts` (ADR 0005, ADR 0011).
- **Settlement (`settle_user` -> `finalize` -> `claim`)** — End-of-cohort permissionless lifecycle after `deadlines[M-1] + grace`: anyone calls `settle_user` once per participant (earning the keeper bounty), then `finalize` once all participants are settled to lock payouts and sweep `fee + dust` to Treasury, after which each participant calls `claim` once (ADR 0013).
- **Keeper** — The unified in-browser auto-sweeper tab that calls permissionless `ChainClient.settleUser` and `ChainClient.finalize` across both `SolanaChain` (Devnet) and `MockChain` (Sandbox). It has no special authority (ADR 0014).
- **Vault** — Per-cohort 0-byte `SystemAccount` PDA (`[b"vault", cohort.key().as_ref()]`) holding pledged native SOL lamports via `SystemProgram::transfer`. The cohort creator seeds its rent-exempt minimum (~`890,880` lamports) at `create_cohort` so `vault_assets = vault.lamports() - rent_min` equals 100% of participant pledges (ADR 0010, ADR 0014).
- **Yield & Lender-Loss Simulation** — Interactive `aprBps` and lender-loss haircut (`h = min(1, A/U)`) controls in `lib/economics.ts` and `MockChain`; the second mock-lender Anchor program is cut from Devnet scope while on-chain `finalize` reads `A = vault.lamports() - rent_min` directly (ADR 0004, ADR 0014).
- **Membership (`Participant` PDA)** — A wallet's participation in one cohort (`[b"participant", cohort.key().as_ref(), user.key().as_ref()]`). A wallet can hold many memberships at once (ADR 0009, ADR 0014).
